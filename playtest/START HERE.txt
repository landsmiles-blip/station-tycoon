==========================================================
 STATION TYCOON — playtest build 2  (November 2, 2026)
==========================================================

NEW IN BUILD 2: the full producer score — real arranged music
that changes with every business. Tell us what you think of it!

Thank you for playing! This is an early test version of a
mobile web game that will be published on YouTube Playables.
Nothing installs. Nothing costs. There are no ads in this
build. Your only job is to play honestly and tell us what
you think — the good, the bad and the confusing.

----------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO OPEN THE GAME
----------------------------------------------------------
The game is the file called:

    Station Tycoon.html

» On a computer (Windows / Mac):
  Double-click that file. It opens in your web browser.
  If it opens in the wrong program, right-click → Open
  with → Chrome (or Edge / Firefox / Safari).

» On an Android phone or tablet:
  1. Unzip this package (long-press the zip → Extract,
     or use the Files app).
  2. Tap "Station Tycoon.html" and choose Chrome.

» On an iPhone or iPad:
  Local files are awkward on iOS. If the file does not open
  in Safari, ask the person who sent you the game for the
  web link version instead — it plays identically.

TIP: A phone in portrait (held upright) is the intended
experience. A computer works too — the game rearranges
itself for wide screens. Turn your SOUND ON; the music and
effects are part of what we are testing.

----------------------------------------------------------
 HOW LONG TO PLAY
----------------------------------------------------------
» Session 1 (today): play at least 10–15 minutes. The game
  has a beginning, a completion moment, and then it keeps
  going — do not stop at the first "well, that looks like
  the end" screen. See what happens if you keep tapping.

» Session 2 (tomorrow, if you can): open the game again the
  next day. There is a once-a-day golden contract and a
  daily streak — we very much want to know whether you
  remembered it, wanted it, or forgot it entirely.

Your progress saves itself automatically, as long as you
reopen the game the same way, in the same browser.

----------------------------------------------------------
 WHAT WE WANT YOU TO NOTICE (do not study it — just play)
----------------------------------------------------------
Play the way you would play anything: no instructions, no
thinking out loud needed. Afterwards, the honest answers to
these are gold to us:

  • Did you understand what to do in the first 30 seconds
    WITHOUT reading anything?
  • Did you ever sit there not knowing what to tap?
  • Did anything look broken, ugly, too small, or unclear?
  • Did the sound work? Did it annoy you or help you?
  • What made you want to keep playing? What made you want
    to stop?
  • Would you open it again tomorrow? Honestly.

Then fill in FEEDBACK.txt (in this same folder) — it takes
about 5 minutes. If typing is a chore, voice notes or a
phone call with the same answers are just as welcome; just
mention "playtest build 1" so we know which version you
played.

----------------------------------------------------------
 IF SOMETHING BREAKS
----------------------------------------------------------
Write down (or screenshot / screen-record):
  1. what you were doing,
  2. what happened instead,
  3. your device and browser.
Bugs are not an inconvenience — in a playtest, a bug is a
gift.

Thanks again. Mean feedback is kind feedback; we would
rather hear it from you now than from a million strangers
later.
